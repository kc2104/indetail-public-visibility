1. The homepage is indexed and currently uses the title "Indetail Design Studio | Interior Designers & Architecture Experts". The page describes the business as an architecture and interior design firm based in Vadodara, Gujarat. The title does not include Vadodara.
2. The homepage displays a placeholder-looking phone number "+01 987654321" as well as +91 84693 01973 and +91 90338 88564.
3. The contact page displays +919879849359 as the primary phone near the top, then lists +91 84693 01973 and +91 90338 88564 in the footer.
4. The homepage has a visible email info@indetail-design.com. The contact page also lists that email.
5. The website has About Us, Get in Touch and Insights/article content. The About page describes residential, commercial, hospitality, landscape, product/furniture and artwork/custom design capabilities.
6. A third-party Vadodara directory currently lists "Indetail Design Studio (Furniture and Product)" with a different address and different contact details (including an old Gmail address). This should be treated as a possible duplicate/inaccurate listing, not copied as canonical business information.
7. A public business data directory identifies the Google Maps entity as Indetail Design Studio, category Architecture firm, 4.9 rating and 32 reviews. Confirmed by the user's supplied Maps share link in chat; no changes have been made to the profile.
8. A public local directory ranking page includes 47 Vadodara businesses with at least five reviews. Its ranking is third-party content, not a Google ranking. Competitors shown include businesses with substantially more reviews, so reviews and prominence are competitive factors.

## Highest-priority technical/content issues

### P0 — Business identity consistency
Do not change or publish a phone/address from third-party directories until the business confirms the canonical values. The public site currently has inconsistent phone numbers. Correct the placeholder number and use one canonical business phone and address consistently across:
- website header/footer/contact page
- Google Business Profile
- Instagram contact buttons/details
- Bing Places / Apple Business Connect
- local directories and business schema

Canonical details must be confirmed by the business owner. Do not infer.

### P1 — Homepage local relevance
Suggested SEO title (draft only; not published):
INDETAIL Design Studio | Interior Designers & Architects in Vadodara

Suggested meta description (draft only; not published):
INDETAIL Design Studio is a Vadodara-based architecture and interior design studio offering residential and commercial interior design, architectural planning and turnkey project services. Explore our work and enquire about your project.

Suggested H1 (draft only; not published):
Interior Design & Architecture Studio in Vadodara

Use the city and service terms naturally. Do not keyword-stuff or claim "best" without substantiation.

### P1 — Dedicated service landing pages
Create distinct, useful pages with original project images and specific scope:
- /interior-design-vadodara/
- /residential-interior-design-vadodara/
- /commercial-interior-design-vadodara/
- /architecture-design-vadodara/
- /turnkey-interior-projects-vadodara/

Do not publish thin duplicate pages. Each page should explain the service, process, deliverables, local project context, FAQs and provide a clear enquiry route.

### P1 — Structured data and crawlability
Check that the site has:
- valid LocalBusiness / ProfessionalService or appropriate business structured data
- consistent name, address, phone and URL in structured data
- Organization/WebSite data where appropriate
- canonical URLs, XML sitemap and robots.txt
- unique title and meta description per indexable page
- descriptive image alt text
- internal links from homepage and relevant service pages
- no accidental noindex directives or blocked important resources

Structured data can help search engines interpret information; it does not guarantee rich results or rankings.

## Measurement plan

Record baseline and then compare monthly:
- Google Search Console: queries, impressions, clicks, CTR, indexed pages and page experience
- Google Business Profile: searches, views, calls, website clicks and direction requests (owner dashboard only)
- manual mobile checks in incognito/private mode, recording date, query, location and whether Maps or organic results were shown
- enquiry source, project type and qualified enquiry count

Do not treat a third-party directory ranking or a single personalised mobile search as a definitive Google ranking.
