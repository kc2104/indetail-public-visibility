## Lead-source tracking fields

For each genuine enquiry, record:
- Date received
- Source platform and profile URL
- Customer's stated project type
- Location / area supplied by the customer
- Budget and timeline only if volunteered
- Public contact route or customer-provided contact details
- Follow-up status
- Outcome

Only record information voluntarily shared for a business enquiry or otherwise lawfully public for business contact. Do not scrape private personal contact details.
